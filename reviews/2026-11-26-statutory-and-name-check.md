### Additional adoption detail

LFDA art. 17 (same consolidation, p. 6) also specifies publication notices including the reserved-rights indication, copyright symbol, holder's full name and address and first publication year (and applicable standard identifier). Omission does not itself extinguish copyright but may carry sanctions for the responsible licensee or publisher. How this applies to a given software or video publication and what appropriate nonresidential contact address can be used should be assessed for that publication. Do not publish a private home address merely on the basis of this note. This is an adoption/compliance issue, not a reason to broaden the PCL restriction.

### Assessment

The verified provisions support the design of the statutory savings clauses, not a guarantee that every PCL provision will be enforceable. Draft 2 already addresses the identified drafting issues. No further substantive license revision is proposed on the basis of these checks. The most important outstanding interpretive uncertainty is whether a given public grant satisfies applicable Mexican written-form requirements and how nonwaivable remuneration and moral rights operate for particular media and distributions.
