## Manual policy walkthrough

These are intended interpretations assuming the actor needs copyright permission and complies with notice conditions. They are not automated legal tests or predictions of judicial outcomes.

| Scenario | Draft 2 result | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Clone repository, compile, modify privately | Permitted | Sections 2 and 3(a) |
| Work on a commercial fork before distribution | Permitted | Sections 2 and 3(a) |
| Independently create a game with identical mechanics | Outside restriction | Sections 1, 3 and 5; no protected expression copied |
| Paid storefront, same game, only logo changed | Not authorized if substitute | All three elements satisfied |
| Original game built using unchanged PCL engine | Permitted | Materially distinct product, sections 1 and 3(e) |
| Same game plus substantial original levels and features | Permitted if independent value is material | Not substantially unchanged |
| Free unmonetized competing mirror | Permitted | No commercial distribution |
| Mandatory donation or paid subscription gate to original copy | Not authorized if unchanged substitute | Commercial distribution defined explicitly |
| Paid bundle containing original game | Fact-specific | Commercial distribution may occur; assess substitution and actual offering |
| Ad-monetized unchanged video reupload | Not authorized if substitute | Public communication and directly attributable revenue |
| Monetized critique with substantial commentary | Permitted subject to rights | Independent value, section 3(f) |
| Commercial translation reaching a new audience | Fact-specific; may be permitted | Audience and independent value matter; no automatic prohibition |

| Genuine paid support for unchanged software | Permitted | Section 3(c) |
| Lawful resale under applicable exhaustion rights | Outside restriction to extent independently lawful | Sections 3 and 5 |
| Paid game causes data loss | Liability limits subject to mandatory law | Sections 6–8 |

## Residual decisions and limitations

- Patent grant remains absent; this may affect recipients' rights if relevant patents exist.
- Voluntary donation-funded mirrors and ordinary hosted execution remain allowed as in Draft 1. No new restrictions on commercial translations are introduced.
- The authorized offering must be publicly available by the challenged distribution; this avoids retroactively making an earlier distribution a substitute based on an offering introduced later. Subsequent distributions must be assessed at their own time. This is a drafting choice, not an established court rule.
- No fixed independent-value threshold, choice of law, arbitration, or indemnity is added. Fact-specific uncertainty is unavoidable.
- A 30-day cure and a per-licensor repeated-breach notice mechanism are retained/refined; their legal effect requires review.
- No independent verification of current statutes or lawyer sign-off was performed in this revision. The source references and historical consolidation caveats in the Draft 1 review remain relevant.
- Formal Mexican copyright/electronic licensing advice, paid-market consumer compliance, name clearance and final release approval remain outstanding. Do not label this version final.
