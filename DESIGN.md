# Design rationale and legal review agenda

## Intended policy

- Broad personal, research, educational and commercial rights.
- Commercial competition through meaningful improvements is expressly allowed.
- Commercial distribution of substantially unchanged direct substitutes is the narrow exception.
- Free redistribution, ordinary commercial component reuse and genuine paid services are permitted.
- No contractual royalties, source disclosure, or mandatory contribution; nonwaivable statutory rights are preserved.
- Attribution and preservation of the applicable grant.
- As-is warranty disclaimer, limitation of liability, and no support obligation, subject to mandatory law.

## Drafting decisions

1. The restriction uses an objective, conjunctive test rather than a general noncompete or a test of the distributor's subjective intent.
2. Direct monetization includes advertising and paid access; unrelated revenue is not automatically caught. Actual distribution costs and genuine services are explicit exceptions.
3. No fixed modification threshold is used; independent value is assessed qualitatively.
4. Downstream recipients obtain covered rights directly from the licensor. New contributions may have separate terms, but rights in covered portions are preserved.
5. No express patent grant, compulsory patent retaliation, choice of law, forum selection, indemnity, or arbitration clause was added. These are deliberate draft defaults, not findings that such terms are unnecessary.
6. A 30-day cure mechanism, severability and continuing permissions are retained from the original proposal.
7. No blanket application of PCL to the repository or other projects is inferred. Draft status is explicit.

## Draft 2 refinements

The [revision assessment](reviews/2026-11-26-draft.2.md) records the response to Draft 1 review findings. Independent creation was already excluded by Draft 1; Draft 2 clarifies, rather than introduces, that protection. The scope of copyright is not enlarged. Commercial development and cloning for development are expressly allowed. The no-express-patent-grant default is retained pending an explicit decision.

## Outstanding legal review

| Area | Question |
| --- | --- |
| Scope and definitions | Are the tests sufficiently definite across software, media, and jurisdictions? |
| Commercial substitution | Can the audience, principal-purpose, and independent-value criteria be applied consistently? |
| Edge cases | How should translations, bundles, paid mirrors, ads, subscriptions, and hosted services be treated? |
| Enforceability | How are copyright conditions distinguished from contractual obligations and competition restrictions? |
| Statutory limits | How do exhaustion, fair use/fair dealing, moral rights, and consumer protections interact with the grant? |
| Warranty and liability | Which exclusions are valid for free distribution and paid products in intended markets? |
| Patents and contributions | Should a separate patent grant or contributor policy be added? |
| Termination | Is notice, cure, and reinstatement language effective and proportionate? |
| Interoperability | How should combined works, package metadata, and platform terms be documented? |
| Naming | Is formal name and trademark clearance needed in intended markets? |

The draft has not been reviewed by counsel, approved by OSI, formally registered with SPDX, or validated in litigation. Public review is welcome. No production readiness is asserted.
